Overview01

Lenders need to spot high-risk borrowers before a loan hits the books. This project builds and compares classifiers that predict default from a borrower’s financial and demographic profile, and just as importantly, explains what actually drives that risk.

How it works02

  1. Explore and prep. Exploratory analysis and preprocessing on the borrower data, with feature engineering on the economic variables.
  2. Compare five models. Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, SVM, and Gradient Boosting are trained and benchmarked.
  3. Evaluate for the real cost. Recall and F1 are prioritized over raw accuracy, because catching an actual defaulter matters more than avoiding a false alarm. Gradient Boosting came out on top.

What it found03

Four factors consistently signalled higher default risk: low checking-account balances, short employment tenure, poor credit history, and a high loan-to-income ratio. That turns a black-box score into something a credit team can actually reason about.
