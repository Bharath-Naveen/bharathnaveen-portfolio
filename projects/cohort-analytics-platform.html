Overview01

Advisors and administrators need to know, at a glance, whether a student is keeping pace toward their degree. This platform compares credits earned against expected pacing and classifies each student as On Track, Behind, or Ahead, then lets staff slice those results by time, organization, and demographic to spot patterns across a whole cohort.

Architecture02

The platform is cloud-native and multi-tier. Data flows through it like this:

  1. ETL. A containerized Julia application (running on ECS / Fargate from an ECR image) ingests student records and runs college- and program-level load jobs.
  2. Compute + store. It calculates degree-progress classification per student and persists results to DynamoDB with secondary indexes tuned for cohort queries; failed records are written to S3 for inspection rather than blocking the job.
  3. API + UI. AWS Lambda functions serve the data per environment to a Vue.js single-page dashboard hosted on S3.

What I built03

I owned the ETL pipeline layer: the containerized load jobs, the degree-progress classification logic (expected pace scaled for semester vs. quarter systems, measured against a configurable tolerance band), the end-to-end data flow into DynamoDB and its indexes, the error-isolation reporting to S3, and multi-environment CI/CD parity through CloudFormation and CodePipeline.

Access note. The platform is owned and operated by the University of Arizona and is access-restricted to university staff. The repository contains no student data, credentials, or internal configuration.
