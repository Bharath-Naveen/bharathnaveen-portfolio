Overview01

First-time student buyers shopping the $5–10k, 2008–2018 used-car market rarely have the automotive know-how to judge which cheap car is a safe bet. AutoRisk turns public safety data into a plain reliability score so they can buy with data-driven confidence instead of a gut feeling.

How it works02

  1. Fetch (NHTSA). Pulls complaints, recalls, and investigation records per vehicle through the NHTSA API, into structured CSVs.
  2. NLP. TF-IDF vectorization with KMeans clustering groups the free-text complaints into recurring failure patterns.
  3. Model. An XGBoost model estimates depreciation and value trajectory.
  4. Score. A scoring engine combines the complaint clusters, recall frequency, and investigation data into one composite risk score, served through a Streamlit app.

Status03

Actively building. AutoRisk is a passion project I keep returning to. The pipeline is modular and Docker-ready, with the fetch, NLP, and scoring stages built out and the interface and modeling being refined.
